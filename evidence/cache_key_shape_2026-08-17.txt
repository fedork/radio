## What the measurement supports

`radiobase.c` already numbers a segment by product first and factorization second; descending
canonical state order is therefore segment mass followed by long side. The mutable solver trie is
rooted by k and then consumes those segment IDs. It does not currently put state length or total
mass before them.

For exact identity, `(segment mass,long side)` determines the complete oriented segment because
the short side is `mass/long`. The user's complete hierarchy is therefore an exact key, although
length and total mass are derived fields rather than additional information.

For dominance, it is not a simple lexicographic-prefix key. A supporting fact may have fewer
segments and lower total mass, so a query needs ranges rather than one exact bucket. The sorted
segment-mass vector is a sound independent necessary-condition profile: an injective
componentwise mapping also maps every segment product to a no-smaller product. The associated long
sides cannot safely be compared lane-by-lane in product order, because the valid injection may
cross those lanes. Example: `(12:1,5:2)` injects into `(7:2,12:1)`, although product-order long
sides `[12,5]` and `[7,12]` fail an associated lane comparison. Independent sorted n, m and product
profiles followed by the existing exact matching test are sound.

The first verifier experiment should therefore add a packed product profile and compare static
orders such as `(np,max-product,total-mass)` and `(np,total-mass,max-product)` on a deterministic
k=7 sample. It should not replace the final injection test. Measure candidates admitted at every
filter, wall time, CPU time and bytes read; require identical statuses, nodes and colored output.

For the mutable solver, current eager dominance closure is already denormalization of implied
facts. The measured last-segment Pareto fronts are the deployed compromise: preserve hot prefix
closure but represent the final dimension as an antichain. A new length/mass outer index should be
tested first as a frozen per-k epoch underneath worker-local exact caches. Materializing the same
implied fact into every compatible length/mass bucket would repeat the memory failure that the
front representation fixed.
