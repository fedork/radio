Coverage-certificate pilot, 2026-09-01
=======================================

Question: could a cleanroom verifier avoid re-searching split space if the frozen refuter
(untrusted producer) emitted its rejection structure as a coverage certificate - per-claim
records annotating every rejected candidate cell (rectangle-merged) plus every descend, in
the LRAT style endorsed by docs/certificate.md:108-116?  This pilot instruments the frozen
refuter with counters only (compile flag RADIO_COVER_PILOT in radiobase.c/radio_refute.c;
the traversal is unchanged - tools/test_radio_refute.sh passes on the instrumented source)
and measures what such a certificate would have to store.

Record classes counted, per audited level k:
  charged/accepted[np]  candidate cells evaluated / descended.  Every descend is one
                        unmergeable `t` record, so accepted is a hard floor on cover size.
  rejected[class]       cells rejected by: cap = prefix-child mass over 3^(k-1) (INFO),
                        rb = suffix reachability, ch0/ch2/ch1 = that prefix child refuted
                        at k-1 by theorem or frozen-trie citation, tail = options retired
                        unevaluated by the monotone counting cut.
  runs[class]           maximal same-class runs in enumeration order - an OPTIMISTIC proxy
                        for merged rectangle records (ch runs may still split by citation).
  pure subtrees         completed subtrees whose rejections are all cap/rb/tail: their
                        validity depends only on (suffix part multiset, remaining cap
                        triple), so equal signatures could share one library entry.
                        Maximal pure subtrees only; duplicate signatures counted per worker
                        (2^20-entry table per worker; saturation reported as drops).

Projection to the full k=7 level (x500; sample prefix count x500 = 3.26e12, consistent with
the recorded 3.22e12):
  accepted `t` records:       ~3.4e11   (hard floor; 7x over the 5e10 GO line by itself)
  rejection-run records:      ~1.8e12   (ch classes merge only ~1.3x even as runs)
  tail-run records:           ~3.4e11
  total verbatim cover:       ~2.5e12 records, multi-TB at any encoding
  library (Tier-3) ceiling:   pure subtrees hold 15% of charged nodes; measured intra-worker
                              signature duplication 12% of those (undercounted by table
                              saturation, but bounded by the 15% regardless)

Verdict: NO-GO for the coverage-certificate architecture, by ~50x against the GO threshold
(5e10 records) and ~5x against the CONDITIONAL ceiling (5e11) with the library lever capped
at 15%.  The run9 k=7 rejection structure is citation-dominated (58% of charged rejections
cite the k-1 trie), prefix-dependent, and does not compress: checking must re-derive it.
Cost of this pilot: instrumentation + two local runs totalling under 12 wall minutes.

Useful positive by-products for the re-search fallback (per-prune win rates at run9 k=7):
  monotone tail cut retires 48x the charged count unevaluated  -> cle prefix tables essential
  per-(part,k) dead options skip 10.1e9 cells per 6.5e9 charged -> Tier-1 bitmap essential
  rb: counterfactual A/B below                                  -> rb can be deferred
  equal-part quotient skips 1.5%                                 -> trivial, keep
  refuter throughput: 15.0M charged cells/s/core on M4 Pro (uninstrumented, run 3 below),
  16M/s/core on c8a                                             -> the parity target
