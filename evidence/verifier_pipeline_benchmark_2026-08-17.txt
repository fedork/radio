## Pipeline and gates

The committed tools/benchmark_verifier_pipeline.sh performs:

1. exact prefix extraction and explicit-root presence checks;
2. provenance build of radio_verify.c;
3. raw-log normalization with CERT_ONLY;
4. a second parse/write pass, required to be byte-identical;
5. same-level minimalization plus top-down coloring from explicit roots;
6. independent replay of the emitted colored certificate;
7. provenance validation, zero-unverified/zero-budget assertions and record-count checks.

Input was the clean, single-session cold bench_sa113_k9.txt:

    SHA-256 85f6a4fb1fd05ccd16a74b1d394c2fcd4c804db6b1a459885af5f94b133f96c6
    315,278 lines; 33,998,104 bytes

It predates embedded provenance and therefore is not trusted as a verdict source. The verifier
independently checks every retained negative fact. The same Sa ladder is also available in the
archived clean 2026 corpus at:
https://github.com/fedork/radio-data/releases/tag/sa-2026-04-27

## Representative replay width comparison

The exact colored certificate was replayed again at 8 and 16 workers. All widths verified the
same 120,537 records and exactly 2,491,817,467 recursion nodes.

workers  CPU mask     wall s  user+system s  peak RSS KiB  memo hit+miss
      8  0-7          434.86        3478.17     1,037,268   8,931,882,315
     14  0-13         369.57        5172.59     1,043,216   9,491,845,978
     16  0-15         347.91        5563.51     1,185,968   9,648,002,282

Fourteen workers save 15.01% wall over eight but consume 48.72% more CPU and issue 6.27% more memo
queries because memo/live/pair caches are worker-local. Sixteen save a further 5.86% wall over
fourteen for 7.56% more CPU. Relative to eight, sixteen save 19.99% wall for 59.96% more CPU.

Measured policy:

- use 16 workers for minimum wall time on an otherwise idle 8-core/16-thread host;
- use 14 when two logical CPUs must remain available; on the shared run9 host, use
  0-6,8-14 if the other workload must have an isolated physical core;
- use 8 one-per-physical-core workers when CPU efficiency matters more than the last 15-20% wall;
- do not optimize the durable text format: sanitize plus round-trip was 0.54 seconds versus more
  than twelve minutes of proof work;
- pre-color minimalization is unequivocally worthwhile, but further coloring work will not remove
  the Sa(113) k=6 bottleneck. The next optimization target is k=6 refutation/index/cache work and
  cross-worker duplication, not parsing or a binary certificate.
