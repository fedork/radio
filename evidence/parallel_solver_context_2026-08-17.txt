Implemented boundary
--------------------

canSolveB_ctx passes one radio_search_context through every recursive B-state call.  The context
owns the accepted-prefix work clock, exact L1, joint-suffix reachability workspace/counters and
negative-verdict counter.  canSolveB is the compatibility wrapper over a default context.

The dominance trie/arenas, split catalog plus s[4]/s[5]/FAST metadata, and sbb_to_min_k remain
mutable process globals.  Concurrent recursive calls are therefore intentionally not enabled.
