# Deadline investigation corrected: preserve the depth-first dive, 2026-08-11

Retraction
----------

The original version of this file called the long `run4`/`run5` child a deadline livelock and
treated commit `c13b5d3` as its repair.  That diagnosis was wrong.  The long child was the intended
depth-first dive, and `c13b5d3` broke the policy that makes such dives useful.  This file supersedes
the mistaken account in place while retaining the observations that led to it.

What the debuggers actually established
----------------------------------------

AWS `run4` (compact cache, frozen bundle `6af384e`) and `run5` (plus exact L1, `290a892`) were both
inside the same information-tight child:

  Sb(9:4,8:4,8:1,5:5,15:1,5:3,6:3,7:2,5:3,11:1,8:2,11:1,14:1,13:1)@5.

Its pair mass is `243 = 3^5`.  Run4 was in exhaustive pass 2 after 144,042,842,325 admitted
prefixes; run5 was in pass 1 after 10,516,061,204.  Both had global
`cant_solve_count=98,355` and local `cant_solve_count_min=98,356`.  Byte-identical debug rebuilds
and read-only GDB attaches support those frames:

  run4 live/debug .text SHA-256  6606d606b5a848927391df65a3b24041d4fa880fa54c42121d2098e802bfa25c
  run5 live/debug .text SHA-256  f3ea042b0b9b2378b52a1a016c1a965bf71e2bcc33e41894401b0cd6f9cee744

The corresponding SSM commands were `39e27445-d0f3-4eaf-89de-55a9e6ee6df7` for the text
comparison and `3fe5a0e3-fc0e-4a9f-8b29-7a2f0ca0f24d` /
`af8cbbcc-a06e-4f88-84f1-ba7642a8a3e6` for the two attaches.  These observations establish where
the processes were; they do **not** establish that the processes were stuck.

The intended state machine
--------------------------

Historical source and commit messages make the control flow explicit:

  * `cant_solve_count_min = cant_solve_count + 1`: a bounded state must contribute negative cache
    facts before it may return `MAYBE`.  Commit `d3a41ed` introduced this as “do not bail out without
    making at least some progress.”
  * While the count is exactly the minimum, the five-times-elapsed grace deliberately slides.  It
    keeps the dive alive until another negative fact is produced.  Once the count exceeds the
    minimum, an expired finite deadline can return `MAYBE`.
  * Pass 1 gives unresolved children the finite local deadline.  Exhaustive pass 2 gives them
    `NO_DEADLINE` (`child_deadline = pass < 2 ? deadline : NO_DEADLINE`).
  * The deadline is tested at complete candidates, immediately before that child call.  It is not a
    general cancellation poll over partial prefixes.

The captured run4-state replay is consistent with this policy: with a nominal five-second parent
budget, the pre-`c13b5d3` engine remained alive beyond 12 CPU seconds and the wrapper stopped it at
17 wall seconds.  That was previously presented as proof of a bug.  It instead shows the required
zero-progress dive refusing to discard its work.

What `c13b5d3` broke
--------------------

`c13b5d3` made two contrary changes:

  * it allowed an expired bounded state to return `MAYBE` with zero negative progress; and
  * it polled every 65,536 admitted prefixes, before a complete candidate reached the pass-2 child
    call.

The second point is decisive.  An expensive pass 1 can expire, enter pass 2, and then bail from the
prefix loop before the first unresolved child is called with `NO_DEADLINE`.  The whole attempt can
therefore return `MAYBE` without leaving a reusable fact.  This explains the `run6` pattern of
multi-thousand-second k=8 attempts followed by no verdict; those absences are `MAYBE`, not
refutations.

Live GDB inspection used byte-identical `-O3 -g` rebuilds of `run3` and `run6`:

  run3 live/debug .text SHA-256  056cd117c37527f9e9d7f7e05d5d09a65d96a0aeeff8a472063acfabfe7c1a73
  run6 live/debug .text SHA-256  3a11395016c75cfd884067a592b78485f7c0d2c0aaae4dc53f26dcf68269f4a3

Both current k=8 size-2 frames were in pass 1 with a finite `parent_deadline` and
`no_deadline=1`, inside unbounded pass-1 work for short states.  Their k=9 roots were also in pass 1.
The stack and locals were captured by SSM commands `d12ad651-6514-46c8-a96c-44adcea4fe54` and
`b2f1a246-ad42-43d0-ba22-3a516f9984e3`.  A focused local debugger stop in the erroneous helper
showed `deadline=200`, `start=100`, `now=201`, `cant_solve_count=0`, and minimum `1`; the helper was
about to treat that zero-progress state as expired.  Together with the source path, this confirms
the broken handoff rather than merely correlating it with the logs.

Restoration and gates
---------------------

Commit `e648e83` removes prefix polling and restores the historical progress condition and sliding
grace.  It retains a small `child_deadline_for_pass` helper so the pass-2 `NO_DEADLINE` handoff is
directly regression-tested.  The relevant gates are:

  * `tools/deadline_regression.c`: zero progress cannot expire; exactly-minimum progress slides the
    grace; progress beyond the minimum can expire; pass 2 maps to `NO_DEADLINE`.
  * The optimized and ASan+UBSan exhaustive split corpora emitted the same 1,038 answers plus
    summary, with output SHA-256
    `5d0ef6f86bc32b7206da6a9dc2f59c06a11e9ac6b1ff6ad97f5788ed22dec6bd`.
  * Against exact pre-change commit `290a892`, split output was byte-identical.  Cache-query outputs
    were also byte-identical at k=5,6,7; their SHA-256 values were respectively
    `b7629d5addfae62ee9e2ab36a71f0d429806e6b006ec2615d2b26d0cda1ebdbb`,
    `1ed2d3c12a6901557f70b80b2ae303b357bec78b36f7892b781f0ead7456a721`, and
    `63a881f51b880bc8e8aa220a339fb54837d52b6070a53cd9953903a0606f4e2b`.
  * `radio_sa193.c` syntax-compiles at both `MAX_N=193` and `MAX_N=1030`; table, witness and
    documentation checks pass.
